Comprehensive Privacy Architecture

This Privacy Policy governs the processing of personal information and telemetry across all games, mobile applications, PC and console software, creator plugins (including Blender add-ons such as Vertex Shadow Painter and Haircards), web platforms, and digital services developed, published, or operated by Mithila Games ("Mithila Games", "Studio", "we", "us", "our"). We are committed to transparency, data minimization, and safeguarding the digital rights of our players and creators worldwide.

1. Scope & Universal Applicability SEC 01

This Privacy Policy applies universally to any user or visitor ("you", "player") who downloads, installs, plays, browses, or interacts with:

Mobile & Handheld Titles

Games and apps published on Google Play Store, Apple App Store, and other authorized mobile marketplaces (including Wings of Paper, Peak, Project X, and future releases).

PC, Console & Web Software

Titles and interactive software distributed via Steam, Epic Games Store, itch.io, native installers (Windows, macOS, Linux), and HTML5 browser builds.

Developer Tools & Addons

Software utilities, 3D artist plugins (e.g., Blender addons), shader packs, and toolsets released directly or via developer asset portals.

Studio Web Properties

Our official domain https://mithilagames.com, community dispatch subscription forms, newsletters, and studio support channels.

2. Categories of Information We Collect SEC 02

We adhere strictly to the principle of data minimization. We only collect information that is strictly necessary to run, optimize, secure, and deliver our games and services.

A. Automatically Collected Telemetry & Diagnostics

When you run our games or software, technical performance metrics are gathered to ensure optimal frame rates, detect crashes, and balance levels:

  • Device model, hardware architecture (CPU/GPU specs, RAM capacity, screen resolution).
  • Operating system name and version (Android, iOS, Windows, macOS, Linux).
  • Runtime engine diagnostics, error logs, and stack traces generated during software anomalies.
  • Anonymized gameplay progression (levels completed, stage durations, achievements unlocked).
  • Truncated or anonymized Internet Protocol (IP) address used to infer coarse regional location (country or city level) for CDN routing and server matchmaking.
B. Device Identifiers & Advertising Tokens

Depending on platform configurations and user consent:

  • Resettable mobile advertising identifiers (such as Google Advertising ID / AAID on Android or Apple Identifier for Advertisers / IDFA on iOS). On iOS, IDFA is only accessed if you grant explicit permission through Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) prompt.
  • Randomly generated application install instance IDs (UUIDs) used to manage local save files, sync settings, or manage game session states without linking to your real-world identity.
C. User-Provided Information

Information you voluntarily communicate to us:

  • Customer Support Inquiries: Your email address and message contents when submitting feedback, reporting bugs, or contacting helpmithilagames@gmail.com.
  • Newsletter Dispatches: Your email address if you voluntarily subscribe to studio updates or devlogs via our website dispatches. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in each email.
  • User Display Names: Public player nicknames or multiplayer handles created inside games supporting leaderboards or multiplayer.
D. In-App Purchases & Financial Information

When you purchase a game, DLC, or virtual item, transactions are processed exclusively by authorized digital storefront operators (Google Play Billing, Apple StoreKit, Steam Microtransactions). Mithila Games does NOT collect, store, or have access to your credit card numbers, bank accounts, or billing addresses. We receive only anonymized confirmation tokens or receipt IDs to unlock your purchased content.

3. Device Permissions & System Access SEC 03

Our applications may request standard operating system permissions to enable specific game functions. These permissions can be reviewed and revoked at any time via your device's system settings:

INTERNET & NETWORK STATE Enables multiplayer connectivity, cloud save syncing, asset streaming, and ad serving.
LOCAL STORAGE / SAVE DIRECTORY Allows the game or tool to read and write local game saves, custom mods, and player preferences.
HAPTICS & VIBRATION Delivers tactile gamepad or touch feedback during gameplay action.
NOTIFICATIONS (OPTIONAL) Delivers gameplay alerts, energy replenishment, or studio updates (only with player consent).

Note: Our games do not access your fine GPS location, microphone, camera, or personal contacts without explicit contextual user action and system authorization.

4. Third-Party SDKs, Analytics & Advertising SEC 04

To support game infrastructure, cross-platform distribution, and sustainable development of free-to-play titles, we integrate with industry-standard third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs). These third parties process data under their respective privacy policies:

Google AdMob / Ad Manager Ad Serving

Serves rewarded videos, interstitial banners, and contextual advertisements. Complies with Google Families Policy in children's applications.

Firebase & Google Analytics Diagnostics

Aggregates anonymous crash reports, app stability metrics, and device hardware distributions to help optimize game performance.

Platform Game Services Social & Saves

Google Play Games Services, Apple Game Center, and Steamworks for player achievements, global leaderboards, and cloud save synchronization.

Game Engine Frameworks Runtime Engine

Core rendering and physics runtimes including Godot Engine, Unity, and Blender APIs. Pure runtime frameworks do not collect personal identifiers.

5. Children's Privacy & Family Compliance (COPPA & GDPR-K) SEC 05

Protecting the privacy of young players is of paramount importance to Mithila Games. We strictly comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) in the United States, GDPR-K in the European Union, the Google Play Families Policy, and the Apple App Store Kids Category guidelines.

  • We do not knowingly collect, harvest, or request personally identifiable information (PII) from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain European jurisdictions).
  • In games specifically designated for children or family audiences, behavioral advertising and interest-based profiling are completely disabled; only contextual, age-appropriate ads from family-certified ad networks are served.
  • If a parent or guardian discovers that a child has provided us with personal information without verifiable parental consent, please contact us immediately at helpmithilagames@gmail.com. We will promptly delete such records from our systems.

6. Data Retention, Storage & Account Deletion SEC 06

We retain gameplay analytics and diagnostic logs only for as long as necessary to analyze game stability, optimize gameplay systems, and resolve technical issues. Support email communications are retained for customer service records and deleted periodically.

// HOW TO REQUEST DATA OR ACCOUNT DELETION:

You have the right to request the permanent erasure of any personal information or telemetry associated with your device or email address. To initiate a deletion request, email helpmithilagames@gmail.com with the subject line "Data Deletion Request" and include your platform/game title. We will process and confirm your request within 30 days without charge.

7. Security Architecture & Safeguards SEC 07

We deploy robust administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. All client-server communications across our websites and web-connected games utilize modern TLS/HTTPS encryption protocols. Access to telemetry repositories and developer accounts is strictly restricted through multi-factor authentication (MFA) and least-privilege security policies.

8. European Union (GDPR) & UK Data Rights SEC 08

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA) or the United Kingdom, you are entitled to specific statutory rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR / UK GDPR):

Right of Access: Request a copy of data we hold about you.
Right to Rectification: Request correction of inaccurate data.
Right to Erasure: "Right to be Forgotten" - request deletion of your data.
Right to Restriction: Request limitation of data processing.
Right to Data Portability: Receive your data in a portable format.
Right to Withdraw Consent: Revoke consent at any time without penalty.

Our lawful bases for processing include: (a) your consent, (b) performance of a contract (providing you with the game and its features), and (c) our legitimate business interests in optimizing gameplay stability and preventing fraud.

9. California Privacy Disclosures (CCPA & CPRA) SEC 09

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act, as amended by the California Privacy Rights Act (CPRA), California residents have specific privacy protections:

  • No Sale of Personal Information: Mithila Games does not sell your personal information to third parties in exchange for monetary compensation.
  • Right to Know & Delete: You may request disclosure of the categories of personal information collected and request its deletion.
  • Non-Discrimination: We will never discriminate against you (by denying services, changing game mechanics, or altering product quality) for exercising your privacy rights.

10. India Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023) SEC 10

In alignment with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, Mithila Games operates as a responsible Data Fiduciary:

  • Personal digital data is collected solely for specified lawful purposes with transparent notice or lawful consent.
  • Data Principals in India retain the right to access summaries of digital personal data, the right to correction and erasure, and the right to nominate a representative in the event of incapacity.
  • For grievance redressal concerning personal data processing in India, please contact our Data Grievance channel at helpmithilagames@gmail.com.

11. How to Control Your Data & Opt Out SEC 11

You have full autonomy over how your devices share information with games and third-party advertising partners:

Android Devices: Open Settings → Google → Ads and tap "Delete advertising ID" or "Reset advertising ID" to clear past ad engagement histories.
Apple iOS / iPadOS Devices: Open Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking and toggle off tracking permissions globally or for specific Mithila Games titles.
Complete Data Cessation: You can immediately stop all active data collection from any application by uninstalling it from your mobile device, console, or computer using the standard OS removal controls.

12. Policy Updates & Studio Contact SEC 12

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to adapt to new legal mandates, evolving game architectures, or updated platform storefront rules. When significant revisions are posted, we will update the version number and Effective Date at the top of this document.
