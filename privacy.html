PRIVACY POLICY

EFFECTIVE DATE: MAY 26, 2025 // VERSION 1.2

This Privacy Policy applies to the Wings of Paper game and associated interactive products (the "Application") developed by Mithila Games ("Service Provider", "we", "us"). The application is provided as a Free service intended for use "AS IS".

1. Information Collection and Use

The Application collects information automatically when you download and play it. This may include:

  • Your device's Internet Protocol (IP) address
  • In-game progression telemetry, session duration, and timestamps
  • Operating system version and device hardware architecture
  • Crash diagnostics and error logs for optimization

The Application does not request or collect precise GPS geographical location data from your mobile device.

2. Third-Party Services

Only aggregated, anonymized telemetry is periodically transmitted to trusted external services to assist us in diagnosing runtime performance and optimizing gameplay systems.

The Application may interface with third-party software development kits (SDKs) that maintain their own privacy policies:

Google AdMob Ad Delivery & Analytics
Godot Engine Runtime Core Game Framework

3. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain gameplay analytics data for as long as you maintain the application on your device and for a reasonable period thereafter. If you wish to request the deletion of any user-submitted feedback or diagnostic data, please contact us directly at helpmithilagames@gmail.com.

4. Children's Privacy

Mithila Games does not knowingly solicit or collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. If we discover that personal data from a child under 13 has been collected without verifiable parental consent, we take immediate measures to delete that information from our servers.

Parents and guardians may contact us at helpmithilagames@gmail.com with any inquiries.

5. Security Standards

We implement physical, electronic, and procedural industry safeguards to protect all telemetry and communication transmitted through our games and website infrastructure.

6. Opt-Out & Uninstallation

You can cease all data processing by the Application at any time by uninstalling the app using standard removal controls available on your device operating system or digital storefront.
